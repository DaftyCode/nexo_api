{{ 'What Nexo keeps on your computer' | transloco }}

{{ 'Nexo keeps what you create. What it keeps by itself has a limit, so it never fills your disk:' | transloco }}

  • {{ 'Request history: 200 entries and 20 MB per workspace, each response cut at 256 KB.' | transloco }}
  • {{ 'Collection runs: the last 20, and 20 MB, per workspace.' | transloco }}
  • {{ 'SQL history: 500 statements. MongoDB query history: 100.' | transloco }}
  • {{ 'Revisions: 25 per document. Cookies: 180 per domain and 3000 per workspace.' | transloco }}
  • {{ 'Desktop log: nexo.log and the one before it, 10 MB at most.' | transloco }}

{{ 'Database results and protocol messages are not kept: they are fetched again. The database of the app gives the disk back the room of what you delete.' | transloco }}
